Your neighbourhood and your room type, not the market median

Every chart on this site reports a market median. A host has a neighbourhood and a room type. This reports the cell you are actually in — and, more usefully, how much that number is worth trusting. Cells with fewer than 10 listings were dropped at build time and never reach this page.
